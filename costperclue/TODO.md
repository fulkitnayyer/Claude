# Cost Per Clue: to-do before launch

The homepage mockup is `costperclue/index.html`. Everything in a dashed red box on that page is a placeholder.

The site speaks as **the Ads Mechanic**, not a personal name or pen name.

## 1. Things only you can supply

- [ ] **Certifications:** the exact name and expiry date (MM/YYYY) of each one. Only list current ones, and set a reminder to update them before they expire. If you'd rather not list any, the certifications tag comes off the page.
- [ ] **Cluster names:** keep or rename *Lead quality*, *Wasted spend* and *Tracking*. The `/fix/…/` web addresses should match whatever you pick.
- [ ] **Newsletter details:**
  - [ ] Keep or change the name "The Clue Sheet".
  - [ ] Choose a send frequency. It replaces `[send frequency]`.
  - [ ] Pick the email service. It provides the form address and the one-click unsubscribe the page promises.
- [ ] **Contact method:** a contact form or an email address.
- [ ] **Exhibit A:** keep the "plumber jobs" example and its $7.40, or supply a different one.
- [ ] **Clue Check content:** the 15–20 questions, how answers are scored, and the fix that goes with each problem the check can find.

## Rules every page must follow

- Never claim "Google Partner" or any Google affiliation. No Google logos or badges.
- Put the independence line, word for word, in the footer of every page: "Cost Per Clue is independent and is not affiliated with, endorsed or sponsored by Google. Google Ads is a trademark of Google LLC."
- No client names, client results, lifetime totals, employer names or sales targets.
- Google Ads only. No Meta or Facebook ads content.
- No invented statistics, testimonials or reviews.
- US English, with $ in all examples. No India-specific pages or copy.
